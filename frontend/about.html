Frequently Asked Questions — About UKA

Everything you need to know about our institute, venue authorization, faculty, and campus location.

Q. Where is UKA Technical Institute located?

Our flagship campus is at House 12, Road Avenue-8, Block M, Banasree, Dhaka (south of Meradia Bazar, directly beside Aarong Shopping Mall, in the UKA School Building). Commute is 3 mins from Rampura Bridge, 4 mins from Aftabnagar, and 6-8 mins from Badda and Khilgaon.

Q. Is UKA an official British Council Computer-delivered IELTS test venue?

Yes. UKA Technical Institute is an authorized British Council testing partner. Candidates can sit both IELTS Academic and IELTS General Training exams on authentic computer terminals with official TRF results.

Q. What courses are offered besides IELTS?

We provide Executive Spoken English with native phonetics, Junior Spoken English for kids (Age 7-12), Secondary English Test (SET) for classes 6-10, Primary English Test (PET) for classes 1-5, and a Basic English Foundation program.

Q. What are the qualifications of your instructors?

Our trainers are Cambridge CELTA / DELTA certified, British Council accredited, and have years of pedagogical experience helping students achieve target band scores of 7.5 to 8.5+.

Q. What is the official fee for Computer-delivered IELTS?

The official test fee is ৳ 31,100 only, strictly matching British Council standard pricing. Additionally, every candidate booking with us receives 5 free full mock tests and 1 month free lab access.

Q. What is UKA's nationwide expansion plan?

UKA Technical Institute is actively working toward establishing modern technical language campuses and test centers across all 64 districts of Bangladesh, expanding access to international-standard education.
