Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Banasree Campus

Answers to common queries from students and test takers across Banasree, Rampura, Badda, and Khilgaon.

Is UKA Technical Institute Banasree an official British Council IELTS Exam Center?

Yes. UKA Technical Institute is an authorized British Council Computer-delivered IELTS Exam Center located at House 12, Road Avenue-8, Block M, Banasree, Dhaka. You can register and sit for both IELTS Academic and IELTS General Training exams right here with an official standard fee of ৳ 31,100.

How do I reach the Banasree campus from Rampura, Badda, Aftabnagar, or Khilgaon?

Our campus is situated in South Banasree, directly beside Meradia Bazar and the Aarong Shopping Mall in the UKA School Building. It is just 3 minutes from Aftabnagar, 5 minutes from Rampura Bridge, 8 minutes from Badda Link Road, and 10 minutes from Khilgaon Taltola and Mouchak Circle. You can take any rickshaw or auto-rickshaw to Meradia Aarong Mall.

What language and IELTS preparation courses are available at the Banasree campus?

We provide comprehensive IELTS Preparation courses (Academic & General with Band 8.5+ trainers), Executive Spoken English for working adults and university students, Junior Spoken English for school kids, Native Pronunciation & Phonetics, and Basic Grammar Foundation courses. Small batch sizes ensure 1-on-1 speaking attention.

Do you offer Junior Spoken English and Phonetics for school students?

Yes! We conduct interactive Junior Spoken English and Spoken Phonetics programs designed specifically for school students from classes 3 to 10. Our trainers utilize Cambridge audio-visual tools, storytelling, speech debates, and phonetics drills to eliminate language hesitation early on.

What benefits do I receive when I register for IELTS at UKA Banasree?

Every candidate who registers for the official ৳ 31,100 IELTS seat at UKA receives 5 Full-Length Computer Mock Tests completely FREE (evaluated by certified examiners) plus 1 Month Unlimited Exam Lab Practice Facility in our main British Council computer lab before their test day.

What are the visiting hours of the Banasree campus?

Our Banasree campus is open Saturday through Thursday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and on Friday from 2:00 PM to 8:00 PM. You can walk in anytime for a free level assessment, campus lab tour, or course consultation.
