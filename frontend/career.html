Faculty & Hiring Guide

Frequently Asked Questions — Careers at UKA

Information regarding faculty recruitment, teacher qualifications, demo classes, and benefits.

Q. What are the qualifications needed to apply as an IELTS Trainer?

Candidates must hold an official IELTS overall score of Band 8.0 or 8.5+, exceptional verbal clarity, and prior coaching experience. Cambridge CELTA/DELTA or British Council accredited teacher credentials receive top priority.

Q. Can I submit my CV even if there are currently no vacancies listed?

Yes! We actively maintain an elite faculty talent pool. When you submit your CV to hr@ukatechnical.com, our academic coordinators keep your profile on file and reach out immediately when new batches launch.

Q. Are there part-time, weekend, or evening batch opportunities?

Yes. In addition to full-time academic leads, we offer flexible evening and Friday-Saturday weekend slots tailored for university professors, corporate trainers, and freelance IELTS examiners.

Q. Where is the UKA campus located for in-person interviews?

Our academic recruitment desk is located at House 12, Road Avenue-8, Block M, Banasree, Dhaka (south of Meradia Bazar, beside Aarong Shopping Mall, in the UKA School Building).
