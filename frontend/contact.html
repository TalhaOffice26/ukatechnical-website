Campus Visit & Location FAQs

Visiting UKA Banasree IELTS Exam Center

Easy directions and guidelines for visitors from Banasree, Rampura, Badda, Aftabnagar, and Khilgaon.

Where is UKA Technical Institute located in Banasree?

We are located at House 12, Road Avenue-8, Block M, Banasree, Dhaka (south of Meradia Bazar, right beside the Aarong Shopping Mall, inside the UKA School Building). Look for the British Council IELTS co-branded signboards.

How do I reach UKA from Rampura Bridge or Hatirjheel?

From Rampura Bridge, take an auto or rickshaw down Banasree Main Road to Meradia Bazar Aarong Shopping Mall. Commute time is approximately 5 to 7 minutes. From Aftabnagar East West University or South Badda, it is a 5-minute ride.

Can I visit the computer testing lab before registering?

Yes! Candidates and guardians are warmly welcome to visit our official British Council exam room, test out the Dell stations and acoustic headphones, and consult with our certified faculty before booking.

What are the admissions and counseling desk hours?

Our admissions desk is open 7 days a week: Saturday through Thursday from 8:30 AM to 8:30 PM, and Friday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
