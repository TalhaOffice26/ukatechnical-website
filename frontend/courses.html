Frequently Asked Questions — Courses & Preparation

Everything you need to know about IELTS coaching, spoken English, phonetics, and kids courses in Banasree, Dhaka.

Where can I prepare for IELTS in Banasree, Rampura, or Badda?

UKA Technical Institute is located at House 12, Road Avenue-8, Block M, Banasree (south of Meradia Bazar, beside Aarong Shopping Mall, UKA School Building). Students from Rampura Bridge, Badda, Aftabnagar, Khilgaon, and Mouchak reach our campus within 3 to 10 minutes. We provide Cambridge-certified mentorship and official mock testing.

How is the Executive Spoken English & Phonetics course structured?

Our Spoken English course covers International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) symbols, reduction rules, syllable stress, intonation, and extempore presentation practice. Designed for university graduates and corporate professionals to eliminate accent hesitation and speak with natural fluency.

Do you have Junior Spoken English classes for children?

Yes! We have specialized batches for Class 1 to Class 5 (PET - Primary English Test) and Class 6 to Class 10 (SET - Secondary English Test). Classes feature gamified storytelling, phonics pronunciation drills, vocabulary expansion, and public speaking confidence building.

What if my English grammar and vocabulary are at a beginner level?

We provide a dedicated Basic English Foundation program. If diagnostic tests show grammar or sentence construction weakness, our instructors guide you through foundational grammar, daily conversational drills, and listening practice before entering rigorous IELTS or advanced spoken modules.

Are computer-delivered mock tests included with IELTS courses?

Yes. Every IELTS preparation student gets weekly full-length computer-delivered mock tests matching real British Council exam screens, complete with one-on-one speaking mock interviews with examiner feedback and band scoring.

Can I attend online classes if I live outside Banasree or Dhaka?

Yes, we offer live interactive Zoom classes with recorded lectures, digital coursework, and 1-on-1 mentor assessment sessions for students across all 64 districts of Bangladesh and overseas expatriates.
