Exam Dates & Booking FAQs

IELTS Test Dates & Venue FAQs (Banasree, Dhaka)

Everything you need to know about scheduling your official British Council exam seat in Banasree.

Where are British Council IELTS exams held in Banasree?

Official Computer-delivered IELTS exams are held at House 12, Road Avenue-8, Block M, Banasree, Dhaka (inside the UKA School Building, right beside the Meradia Bazar Aarong Shopping Mall). The venue is very close to Rampura Bridge, Badda, and Aftabnagar.

What are the Morning and Day exam slot timings?

Morning Slot: Listening, Reading, Writing 9:00 AM – 11:45 AM, Speaking in the afternoon. Day Slot: Speaking from 9:00 AM, Listening, Reading, Writing 1:00 PM – 3:45 PM. You complete all 4 skills on the exact same day.

Can candidates from Rampura, Badda, Aftabnagar, or Khilgaon sit exams here?

Yes! Candidates living across East Dhaka (Rampura, Badda, Hatirjheel, Aftabnagar East West University, Khilgaon, Mouchak) prefer UKA Banasree because it eliminates exhausting travel on exam morning.

What documents must I bring on exam day?

You must bring your original, valid Passport (the exact passport used during your online British Council registration). No other identification or photocopies are accepted under British Council global test security protocol.
