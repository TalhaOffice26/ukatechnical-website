Banasree IELTS Exam Center & British Council Venue

Where is the official British Council IELTS exam center located in Banasree?

UKA Technical Institute is located at House 12, Road Avenue-8, Block M, Banasree, Dhaka (south of Meradia Bazar, right beside the Aarong Shopping Mall, inside the UKA School Building). We are the central test venue serving Banasree, Rampura Bridge, Badda, Aftabnagar, Khilgaon, and Mouchak.

Why is taking the IELTS exam at UKA Banasree better than distant centers in Dhanmondi or Uttara?

For candidates living in East Dhaka (Banasree, Rampura, Badda, Aftabnagar, Khilgaon), commuting across town on exam morning introduces heavy traffic delays and fatigue. Sitting your exam right in Banasree allows you to arrive fresh, well-rested, and confident with zero travel stress.

What are the test slot timings at UKA Banasree IELTS Exam Center?

We provide two daily sessions: Morning Slot (Listening, Reading, Writing 9:00 AM – 11:45 AM, Speaking in the afternoon) and Day Slot (Speaking from 9:00 AM, Listening, Reading, Writing 1:00 PM – 3:45 PM). You complete all 4 modules on the same day.

What is the official British Council IELTS exam fee and what free perks are included?

The official British Council fee is ৳ 31,100 for Computer-delivered IELTS (Academic and General Training). When registering your exam date through UKA Technical Institute, you receive 5 Full-length Computer Mock Tests FREE (worth ৳ 3,000) and 1 Month Free Lab Practice access.

Can candidates and guardians inspect the computer testing lab before registering?

Yes! Candidates and guardians are warmly welcome to visit our official British Council computer room 7 days a week (Saturday-Thursday 8:30 AM – 8:30 PM, Friday 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM). You can test out the Dell stations, acoustic headphones, and consult with certified faculty before booking.
