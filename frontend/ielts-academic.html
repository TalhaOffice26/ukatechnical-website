Section 1 ⏱ 30 Mins + 10 Mins Transfer

Listening Module

You will listen to four recordings of native English speakers and then write your answers to 40 questions. The recordings include conversations in everyday social contexts, university lectures, and group educational discussions.

Section 2 ⏱ 60 Minutes

Academic Reading Module

Consists of three long texts ranging from descriptive and factual to discursive and analytical. Passages are authentic texts taken from research books, journals, magazines and newspapers appropriate for candidates entering university.

  • Matching headings and information to paragraphs
  • True / False / Not Given & Yes / No / Not Given statements
  • Sentence completion, diagram labeling and summary completion
Section 3 ⏱ 60 Minutes

Academic Writing Module

Topics are of general interest to candidates entering undergraduate or postgraduate studies:

Task 1 (150 words - 20 mins):

You are presented with a graph, table, chart, process diagram or map and asked to describe, summarize or explain the information in your own words.

Task 2 (250 words - 40 mins):

You are asked to write a formal discursive essay in response to a point of view, argument or problem. Must show coherent logic and evidence.

Section 4 ⏱ 11 – 14 Minutes

Speaking Module

A real-time, interactive spoken interview with an authorized British Council examiner. It is recorded and split into 3 parts: general introduction, 2-minute cue card presentation, and in-depth conceptual discussion.

How Examiners Score Your Academic Writing & Speaking

1. Task Achievement

Did you answer every aspect of the question and clearly summarize key trends?

2. Coherence & Cohesion

Logical paragraphing, transition connectors and seamless idea progression.

3. Lexical Resource

Sophisticated, precise academic vocabulary, collocations and idiom range.

4. Grammatical Range

Mix of complex structures, correct passive voice, conditionals and punctuation.
