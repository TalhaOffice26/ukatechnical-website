IELTS Exam Center FAQs (Banasree, Rampura, Badda)

Official British Council Computer-delivered IELTS test venue guidelines, test fees, and candidate facilities.

Where is the official British Council IELTS exam center in Banasree?

UKA Technical Institute is located at House 12, Road Avenue-8, Block M, Banasree, Dhaka, right beside the Meradia Bazar Aarong Shopping Mall, in the UKA School Building. It is the central test venue serving Rampura, Hatirjheel, Badda, Aftabnagar, Khilgaon, and Mouchak.

What is the official fee for Computer-delivered IELTS?

The standard fee for both IELTS Academic and IELTS General Training (Computer-delivered) is ৳ 31,100 only. Candidates pay the exact authorized British Council fee without any hidden consultation or processing markup.

What are the benefits of booking IELTS at UKA Technical Institute?

All candidates booking their IELTS seat with us receive 5 Full-Length Computer Mock Tests with detailed feedback + 1 Month Full Access to our computer practice lab totally FREE before test day.

Why is each test slot limited to strictly 10 candidates?

To eliminate acoustic overlap, guarantee zero keyboard noise distraction, and ensure dedicated invigilator attention, we limit each session (Morning 09:00 AM & Day 01:30 PM) to strictly 10 seats.

When are the computer-delivered IELTS results published?

Computer-delivered IELTS results are released within 3 to 5 calendar days. Candidates can check their electronic TRF online immediately and collect physical certificates without long delays.

Can I choose my Speaking interview time on the same day?

Yes. The in-person speaking interview is held in our private acoustic speaking suite on the same test day, allowing you to complete all four modules (Listening, Reading, Writing & Speaking) in one convenient visit.
