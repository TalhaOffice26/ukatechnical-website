Section 1 ⏱ 30 Mins (+10 Mins Transfer)

Listening Module

The Listening test is identical in both IELTS Academic and General Training. You listen to 4 audio tracks (conversations, everyday inquiries, instructions, and talks) and complete 40 questions.

Section 2 ⏱ 60 Minutes

General Reading Module

Unlike Academic Reading, General Reading focuses on everyday survival skills and workplace documents:

Section 1 (Social Survival):

2-3 short factual texts such as timetables, hotel brochures, public notices, and advertisements.

Section 2 (Workplace Survival):

2 texts focusing on the workplace, including job descriptions, staff training contracts, workplace safety policies, and company handbooks.

Section 3 (General Reading):

1 longer passage involving descriptive and instructional prose on a topic of general public interest.

Section 3 ⏱ 60 Minutes

General Writing Module

Task 1 (150 words - 20 mins):

You are presented with a real-life situation and required to write a letter asking for information or explaining a situation. The letter may be formal (to a manager/landlord), semi-formal (to a colleague), or informal (to a close friend).

Task 2 (250 words - 40 mins):

Write an argumentative or opinion essay presenting arguments, solutions, and justifications. Evaluated with equal weight to Task 1.

Section 4 ⏱ 11 – 14 Minutes

Speaking Module

Face-to-face interactive interview identical to Academic Speaking. Assessed on Fluency and Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range and Accuracy, and Pronunciation.
