Frequently Asked Questions — IELTS Center Banasree

Everything you need to know about taking and preparing for your IELTS exam in Banasree, Dhaka.

Where is the official British Council IELTS exam center located in Banasree, Dhaka?

UKA Technical Institute is located at House 12, Road Avenue-8, Block M, Banasree, Dhaka (south of Meradia Bazar, right beside the Aarong Shopping Mall, inside the UKA School Building). We are the central authorized British Council test venue serving Banasree, Rampura Bridge, South Badda, Aftabnagar (East West University), Khilgaon, and Mouchak.

Which is the best IELTS preparation coaching center near Banasree, Rampura, or Badda?

UKA Technical Institute is widely recognized as the premier IELTS preparation center in East Dhaka. We feature Cambridge certified master trainers, small interactive batches (max 15 students), 1-on-1 speaking evaluations, regular mock tests inside the official British Council exam lab, and 6-day practice access for all enrolled students.

Can I take Computer-Delivered IELTS mock tests in the actual British Council exam lab?

Yes! UKA candidates practice inside the authentic exam room using genuine test software, Dell Core-i5 stations, and noise-isolating headsets. We offer single mocks (৳ 750) and discounted packages with complete band score reports and certified examiner feedback delivered within 24 hours.

What is the official British Council IELTS exam fee and what free perks are included?

The standard British Council fee for Computer-delivered IELTS (Academic or General Training) is ৳ 31,100 only. When you book your test seat through UKA Technical Institute, you receive 5 Full-length Computer Mock Tests FREE (worth ৳ 3,000) and 1 Month Unlimited Exam Lab Practice totally free.

Why should candidates from Aftabnagar, Rampura, Khilgaon, and Badda choose UKA Banasree?

Instead of traveling through heavy Dhaka traffic to Dhanmondi or Uttara on test morning, candidates in East Dhaka can sit their official exam right here in Banasree with zero travel fatigue. Morning (9:00 AM) and Day (1:30 PM) slots allow you to complete Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking all on the same day.

How do I reach UKA Technical Institute in Banasree from Rampura Bridge or Hatirjheel?

From Rampura Bridge, take an auto-rickshaw or rickshaw straight into Banasree Main Road to Meradia Bazar Aarong Shopping Mall (Block M, Avenue-8). It takes roughly 5 to 7 minutes. From Aftabnagar (East West University) or South Badda, it is a quick 5-minute commute.
