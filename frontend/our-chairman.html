Honorable Chairman
UKA Group Leadership

Engr. Md. Faruk Hossain

Founder & Honorable Chairman, UKA Group & UKA Technical Institute

"At UKA Technical Institute and UKA Group, our core mission is to empower the young minds of Bangladesh with world-class language fluency, official British Council certifications, and moral leadership. We believe quality education is the ultimate catalyst for personal transformation and national progress."

Under his visionary guidance, UKA has grown from a humble beginning into a premier educational powerhouse comprising British Council authorized testing facilities, high-performance IELTS laboratories, and UKA Collegiate School.

His relentless emphasis on state-of-the-art computer infrastructure, small student batches, and Cambridge-certified trainer mentorship continues to set benchmark standards for English language education in Dhaka and throughout Bangladesh.
