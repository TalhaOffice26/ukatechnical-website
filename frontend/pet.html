Progression Road

3 Developmental PET Milestones

Diagnostic Placement Test Included
Level 1 • Ages 6-7

Phonics & Discovery

Single phonemes, 100 high-frequency sight words, everyday greetings and object naming.

Milestone: Read simple 3-4 letter sentences smoothly.
Level 2 • Ages 8-9

Conversational Explorer

Past/present tense usage, question construction, role plays (restaurant, shopping, doctor, school).

Milestone: Speak spontaneously in full conversational paragraphs.
Level 3 • Ages 10-11

Junior Communicator

Short chapter comprehension, creative descriptive stories, junior debates and seamless bridge to SET.

Milestone: Write multi-paragraph essays and give presentations.
British Council & Cambridge Benchmark

Official British Council Primary English Test (PET)

International Young Learners English (YLE) assessment standards designed to give children a joyful headstart.

CEFR Pre-A1 to A2 No Fail Stigma
1

Listening Assessment

Duration: ~20-25 Minutes (4-5 Parts). Children listen to friendly native British audio tracks, draw lines matching names to illustrated characters, write simple numbers, and follow color instructions.

2

Reading & Writing

Duration: ~20-40 Minutes (5-7 Parts). Children match pictures to vocabulary words, verify True/False statements, complete short story gaps with word banks, and write full descriptive sentences.

3

1-on-1 Speaking Test

Duration: ~3-9 Minutes. A warm, face-to-face conversation with a friendly certified examiner using colorful scene cards, 'spot-the-difference' games, and simple questions about their school, family, and hobbies.

Official British Council 5-Shield Certificate (No Pass or Fail)

Every participating child receives an authentic British Council / Cambridge Assessment English certificate. Instead of pass/fail grades, performance is awarded in shields (up to 5 shields per skill), ensuring every young learner feels celebrated, motivated, and inspired to continue their language journey.
