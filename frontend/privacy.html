1. Information We Collect

When you register for an official British Council IELTS examination, schedule a full-length mock test, or enroll in academic courses at UKA Technical Institute, we collect necessary candidate identification information including your full legal name, passport number, phone number, email address, and academic background.

2. British Council Compliance & Test Administration

As an official British Council examination venue, all candidate verification documents and biometric data are processed in strict compliance with British Council international testing protocols and data security mandates. Candidate identification data is transmitted securely for the sole purpose of exam registration and issuing Test Report Forms (TRF).

3. How We Use Your Data

We utilize your contact information solely to communicate exam schedules, morning/day slot allocations, venue announcements, mock test performance evaluations, and relevant course updates. We enforce a zero-spam policy and never sell or rent your personal information to third-party commercial marketing entities.

4. Data Security & Storage

All sensitive student credentials and passwords stored within our web portal are secured using industry-standard cryptographic hashing (Bcrypt) and protected HTTP-only cookie sessions to prevent unauthorized access.

5. Candidate Rights & Contact

You have the full right to inspect, update, or request the deletion of your personal account information from our system at any time by contacting our central administration office at info@ukatechnical.com or via our campus reception desk.
