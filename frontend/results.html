IELTS Test Results & TRF Collection

Everything you need to know about accessing your official British Council IELTS scores online, understanding your Test Report Form (TRF), and collecting your physical certificate.

3 to 5 Days Result Computer-Delivered IELTS

IELTS on Computer Results

If you took IELTS on Computer at our lab, your official band scores are typically published online within 3 to 5 calendar days from your test date. You will receive an SMS and email notification directly from the British Council.

Check Computer Result Portal ↗
13 Calendar Days Paper-Based IELTS

IELTS on Paper Results

For candidates who sat the traditional paper-based exam, results are previewed online exactly on the 13th calendar day following your test date. Your physical Test Report Form (TRF) is printed and dispatched simultaneously.

Check Paper Result Portal ↗

Physical TRF Certificate Collection

You can collect your official paper Test Report Form (TRF) directly from the UKA Technical Institute Exam Reception during business hours:

  • Bring your original passport: Certificates are strictly handed over after verifying your original passport.
  • Authorized representative: If sending a family member, they must carry a signed authorization letter, candidate's original passport, and their own NID/Passport.
  • Courier delivery: If you selected courier delivery during registration, tracking details will be texted to your mobile phone.
Score Dispatch

Sending TRFs to Universities

During registration, you can nominate up to 5 universities or immigration bodies to receive your official electronic TRF completely free of charge.

Not Satisfied with Your Band Score?

You have the option to apply for an Enquiry on Results (EOR / Remarking) within 6 weeks, or register for IELTS One Skill Retake.
