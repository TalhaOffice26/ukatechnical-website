British Council & Cambridge Benchmark

Official British Council Secondary English Test (SET)

Standardized B1 Preliminary & B2 First assessment framework preparing teens for board exams and early IELTS Band 7.5+.

CEFR B1 to B2 Pre-IELTS Pathway
1

Reading Comprehension

Duration: 45 Minutes (6 Parts). Evaluates reading for gist, detailed comprehension, public notices, brochures, newspaper articles, multiple choice, and open cloze grammatical accuracy.

2

Writing Paper

Duration: 45 Minutes (2 Tasks). Task 1: formal/informal email (~100 words). Task 2: choice of story or structured article (~100 words). Marked on content, organisation, and vocabulary range.

3

Listening Test

Duration: ~30 Minutes (4 Parts). Candidates listen to short everyday announcements, interviews, and discussions with native English accents, answering multiple-choice and notes-completion prompts.

4

Paired Speaking Test

Duration: 10-12 Minutes. Taken in pairs with two examiners. Covers candidate interviews, individual photograph descriptions, interactive partner tasks, and discussions on teen topical issues.

Official British Council / Cambridge Statement of Results

Candidates receive an internationally recognized Statement of Results and Certificate mapping directly to the Common European Framework of Reference (CEFR Levels A2, B1, and B2). This certificate acts as an irrefutable credential for English-medium school admissions, scholarship applications, and gives a massive headstart for IELTS.
