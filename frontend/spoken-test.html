What Happens During the 15-Minute Test?

Conducted in an acoustic cabin in a relaxed, friendly environment:

1. Casual Dialogue

Initial 4-5 minutes chatting about your daily life, profession, hobbies, and background to establish spontaneous fluency.

2. Topic Presentation

You speak for 2 minutes on an unfamiliar topic to evaluate structured narrative building, vocabulary depth, and coherence.

3. Abstract Discussion

Exploring opinions on social, educational or technology trends to assess complex grammar and argument formulation.

4. Instant Debrief

The evaluator provides your score breakdown, pinpoints pronunciation issues, and maps out your exact course requirements.
