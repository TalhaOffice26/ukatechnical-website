1. Course Enrollment & Fees

Admission to any UKA Technical Institute course (including IELTS Masterclass, Spoken English, SET, or PET) is confirmed upon registration and tuition verification. All course materials, diagnostic evaluations, acoustic booth access, and computer lab slots are included in the declared course fee.

2. British Council Exam Registration Policies

Candidates registering for an official British Council IELTS examination through UKA must present an authentic, valid original Passport. Test fees, test dates, cancellation rules, and medical refund procedures are governed strictly by British Council national regulations.

3. Mock Test Attendance & Rescheduling

Candidates booking mock test slots must arrive 20 minutes prior to the start of the listening section. If you wish to reschedule a mock test date, our admissions desk must be informed at least 24 hours in advance to transfer your seat without additional charge.

4. Campus Code of Conduct & Lab Equipment

UKA Technical Institute maintains a safe, focused, and professional learning environment. Candidates must treat all audio headsets, computer peripherals, and testing stations with diligence. Any disruption to exam halls or intentional damage will lead to immediate cancellation of student status without refund.
